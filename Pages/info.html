Community Rules

To keep Aura a safe place for creators, we ask everyone to follow these simple guidelines:

  • 01 Respect the Craft: Be kind to other creators. Constructive feedback is welcome, hate speech is not.
  • 02 Credit the Source: If you share someone else's work, always provide a link to the original creator.
  • 03 Keep it Cozy: Aura is a "night-mode" community. Avoid spamming or high-frequency automated posting.
  • 04 Moderation: Community owners have the final say in their respective feeds.

Support & System

Aura is currently in Developer Preview. If you encounter a bug or a "robot" behaving badly:

  • Reports can be sent via the Admin Dashboard.
  • Check the System Status for server maintenance updates.

Privacy & Terms

We believe in privacy. Aura does not track your data for ads. Your posts are yours, and you can delete your account and data at any time from the settings menu.
